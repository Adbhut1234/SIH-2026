## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor of institutional real-estate underwriting and cadastral law merged with the surgical precision of modern document intelligence. Designed for legal counsels, title insurance underwriters, and sovereign land registry officers, the interface communicates absolute fidelity, structural permanence, and machine-learned clarity.

The visual style is **Elevated Modern Fintech Craft**:
- **Parchment Substratum**: A crisp warm-gray canvas (`#F8F9FA` to `#F1F3F5`) providing a tactile, paper-adjacent ground without feeling dated.
- **Obsidian Anchor Points**: Primary command surfaces, navigational hubs, and dominant calls-to-action utilize deep obsidian slate (`#0F172A`) to ground the layout and decisively dictate user forward momentum.
- **Architectural Skeuomorphic Touch**: Micro-tactile inner shadows, extruded debossed record gutters, and micro-bevels provide physical reassurance that data fields are verified, unchangeable, and locked.
- **Crystalline State Accents**: Verification confidence levels bypass ornamental decoration; status markers rely on luminous jewel-grade emeralds for deterministic AI matches and amber topaz for boundary or legal discrepancies.
- **Intentional Density**: Data density is embraced rather than hidden, managed strictly through progressive disclosure, tabular tabular numerics, and explicit spatial grouping.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated chromatic hierarchy designed to reduce optical fatigue during extended audits while creating unmistakable action pathways.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate)**: The structural anchor. Reserved for primary operational buttons, active workspace tab strokes, key cadastral layer triggers, and supreme headers. It commands immediate focus.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Emerald Assurance)**: The visual badge of ground truth. Applied strictly to confidence meters exceeding 98%, signed cryptographic seals, and completed parcel reconciliations.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Amber Verification Trigger)**: Alerts underwriters to ambiguous metes-and-bounds records, missing lien satisfactions, or low OCR certainty thresholds. Demands manual confirmation.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#64748B` - Slate Core)**: Bridges the contrast between deep text and neutral backdrops.
  - Base Surface: `#F8F9FA` (Warm Parchment Gray)
  - Raised Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Card White)
  - Sunken/Debossed Inset Surface: `#EEF0F2` (Field Track Gray)
  - Hairline Borders: `#E2E8F0` (Structural Frame)
  - Critical Alert / Deed Encumbrance: `#DC2626` (Crimson)

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Hanken Grotesk** for primary brand architecture, parcel indices, and operational section titles, lending geometric order and structural weight. **Geist** delivers uncompromised legibility for nested JSON extractions, legal deed descriptions, parcel metes and bounds, and tabular ledger views.

- **Tabular Figures**: All data components, coordinates, parcel IDs, and currency amounts must explicitly activate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical balance across comparative document panels.
- **Labeling Discipline**: Small labels (`label-sm`) require slight positive tracking (`0.04em`) and uppercase styling when designating automated AI states or jurisdiction codes.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Every screen permits strictly one `headline-xl` or `display-lg`. Secondary extractions downscale to `headline-sm` with muted slate tones (`#64748B`) to keep attention focused on primary workflow differentials.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface implements an intentional split-plane viewport model explicitly tuned for concurrent document-to-data reconciliation:

- **The Split Stage Architecture**:
  - **Left Viewport (50-60%)**: High-resolution raster or vector legal deed viewer, GIS parcel overlay, and historical boundary map viewer.
  - **Right Viewport (40-50%)**: The extraction pipeline, entity resolution trees, title deed discrepancies, and immediate sign-off cards.
- **Spacing Scale & Rhythms**: Built upon a strict 4px base rhythm:
  - Micro spaces (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) govern form groups, token tags, and entity pairings.
  - Component interior padding relies on `space-lg` (16px) for compact precision and `space-xl` (24px) for elevated operational cards.
  - Structural margins between panes default to `space-xl` (24px) on desktop workspaces.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (>1440px)**: Dual-pane synchronized scrolling with pinned primary actions.
  - **Tablet / Small Laptops (1024px - 1439px)**: Collapsible sidebar navigation; document canvas collapses to 40% with a slide-over comparison toggle.
  - **Mobile (<1023px)**: Single column stack. The UI auto-toggles between "Original Document" and "Extracted Model" via a segmented floating obsidian control.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids exaggerated blur shadows in favor of **tactile, soft-inset bevels and multi-layered planar lighting** that evokes etched drafting film, milled slate, and parchment surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation)**: `#F8F9FA`. Base page canvas. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Extruded Cards & Workspaces)**: `#FFFFFF`. Casts an ambient dual shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Outlined with a microscopic stroke: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspection Flyouts & Hover Panels)**: Elevated surface casting soft ambient depth: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Command Trays & Modal Verifications)**: Floating action docks: `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **The Tactile Inset (Debossed Field Cavity)**: Used for data input cavities, unedited OCR fields, and confidence meters: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Creates an architectural impression of cut physical grooves on the card surface.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 1 (Soft)** corner geometry. Rounded corners are controlled, sharp, and architectural, reinforcing legal authority and enterprise precision:

- Standard Controls & Fields: `0.25rem` (4px).
- Container Cards, Table Wrappers, Split Panels: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modal Dialogs, Stage Overlays: `0.75rem` (12px).
- Badges & Metric Tokens: Retain matching precision `0.25rem` (4px) or strict circular tags for status indicators (`9999px` strictly for dot indicators).
- Borders: Crisp, hairline-weighted 1px boundaries (`#E2E8F0` resting, `#CBD5E1` active, `#0F172A` focused).

## Components

### Buttons & Primary Call-to-Action Architecture
- **Primary Operational Action ("Make Next Action Obvious")**: Obsidian black solid background (`#0F172A`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` border-radius. Inset highlight stroke: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a subtle forward arrow or tactile hotkey badge (`⌘ + ↵`).
- **Secondary Action**: White surface, hairline border (`#CBD5E1`), dark slate text (`#1E293B`). Soft hover background (`#F8F9FA`).
- **Verification CTA**: Emerald background (`#059669`), white text, used strictly when executing final commit to the land records ledger.

### Extraction Chips & Status Indicators
- **Verified Confidence Chip**: `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` typography, `#A7F3D0` micro-border. Prefixed with a 6px solid emerald dot.
- **Attention Needed Chip**: `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` typography, `#FDE68A` micro-border. Displays discrepancy count (e.g., `! 2 Mismatches`).
- **Processing / Extraction Chip**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, animated 1.5s subtle pulse.

### Form Fields & Editable Insets
- Resting fields use the debossed cavity styling: `#F8F9FA` background with a subtle inner shadow.
- On focus: Background elevates to pure white (`#FFFFFF`), with an outer ring of `2px solid #0F172A` with a 1px white offset.
- Verified AI Extractions show a trailing locked shield or emerald checkmark; clicking opens the source bounding box in the left viewport.

### Comparison Lists & Audit Rows
- Alternating subtle line separation (`1px solid #F1F5F9`).
- Hovering a record highlights the corresponding metes-and-bounds polygon or legal text highlight in the document panel with a soft amber-tinted box (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`).

### Cadastral Verification Cards
- White background card with Level 1 elevation.
- Header contains Parcel Identification Number (PIN) in `mono-data` with an obsidian copy trigger.
- Body displays a structured key-value matrix with vertical divider lines.
- Card footer anchors contextual guidance: e.g., *"Deed grantor matches 1994 title chain. Press Verify to endorse."*
